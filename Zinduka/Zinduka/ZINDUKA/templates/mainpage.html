ABOUT US

WHO ARE WE?

Zinduka is a movement dedicated to ending femicide and all forms of violence against women and girls. We aim to do awaken communities to speak out, take action, and protect lives. We raise awareness, support survivors, and push for change through education, advocacy, and digital campaigns. At Zinduka, we believe every woman deserves to live free from fear.

MISSION AND VISION

OUR MISSION

"Empowering individuals to end femicide and GBV through safe reporting, awareness and support"

OUR VISION

"A Kenya free from femicide and gender-basedviolence, where every woman and girl, regardless of age and background, feels safe, empowered and heard!"

Zinduka is a safe space where survivors turn pain into power. Built to fight femicide, it allows women to report danger and share their stories — not as victims, but as voices of strength. Every report honors those we have lost and protects those still at risk. Here, silence ends, healing begins, and every woman is reminded: your voice matters, your life matters — Zinduka, rise.

ZINDUKA

Zinduka is a safe space for survivors of gender-based violence in Kenya. We amplify voices, provide support, and work to end violence against women and girls.
